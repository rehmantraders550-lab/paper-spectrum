# Paper Spectrum — Material Imagery System v1

## Purpose

Paper is the subject, not the background. Every image must reveal a measurable or tactile property of the sheet: surface, shade, formation, edge, bulk, fold, print response, finishing response, or application.

The imagery must preserve the existing Paper Spectrum editorial system: warm paper canvas, restrained serif/sans hierarchy, 1px rules, square geometry, generous negative space, and a calm material-study tone.

## Core image families

### 1. Hero Sheet
Purpose: establish the material as an object.

- One or two sheets only.
- Strong negative space.
- Visible curl, edge, or fold.
- Raking natural/studio light.
- No lifestyle props.
- Preferred crop: 4:5 or 3:2.
- The sheet must remain identifiable without captions.

### 2. Surface Macro
Purpose: prove texture and fibre.

- Macro or close-focus framing.
- Show grain, tooth, laid/felt marks, coating, fibre inclusions, shimmer, or surface irregularity.
- Light should travel across the surface, not flatten it.
- Preferred crop: 1:1 or 4:5.
- Do not exaggerate texture beyond physical plausibility.

### 3. Stack Edge
Purpose: communicate bulk, caliper, density, and edge character.

- 8–30 sheets in a disciplined stack.
- Side light across edges.
- Avoid perfect CGI repetition.
- Show minute edge variation where appropriate.
- Preferred crop: 4:5 or 16:10.

### 4. Transformation
Purpose: show how the paper behaves when converted.

Examples:
- fold / score
- emboss / deboss
- foil
- letterpress
- offset ink
- spot UV
- die-cut edge
- binding / stitching

The process must remain physically believable. Never combine incompatible finish behavior merely for drama.

### 5. Application
Purpose: show what the stock can become.

Use sparingly:
- invitation
- editorial spread
- packaging
- tag
- stationery
- presentation object

The material must still dominate the frame. Avoid generic lifestyle scenes.

## Visual hierarchy

Object → Surface → Edge → Transformation → Application → Specification

Do not lead with decorative context.

## Lighting

Primary:
- directional side/raking light
- soft but defined shadow
- neutral-to-warm daylight
- high local texture readability

Avoid:
- neon
- colored rim lights
- excessive bloom
- glossy CGI highlights on uncoated stock
- dramatic smoke/haze
- hard speculars unless the substrate genuinely supports them

## Camera behavior

- Orthographic or long-lens visual discipline for sheet/stack studies.
- Macro perspective for surface studies.
- Minimal depth-of-field; enough surface must stay readable.
- No dutch angles.
- No exaggerated wide-angle distortion.

## Color

UI remains:
- Paper #F7F5F0
- Ink #151514
- Paper Mist #ECE9E2
- Spectrum Wash #D9DEE0

Photography may carry material color, but should remain naturally graded.

## Composition

Preferred:
- one dominant paper gesture
- 60–80% quiet field
- strong edge geometry
- asymmetry governed by the 12-column system
- editorial crop rather than ecommerce packshot

Avoid:
- crowded tabletop scenes
- swatch fans as the default visual
- repeated “stack of paper on beige table” compositions
- decorative stationery props
- flowers, coffee cups, pencils, spectacles, or generic studio accessories

## Material Detail sequence

Each material detail page should target six evidence frames:

01 — Hero Sheet
02 — Surface Macro
03 — Stack Edge
04 — Transformation
05 — Application
06 — Technical / comparison frame

A material may launch with fewer frames, but the sequence order should remain stable.

## Authenticity gates

An image fails if:
- paper looks like plastic, fabric, stone, or leather without justification
- surface relief is physically implausible
- sheet thickness does not match the represented weight
- edges are perfectly cloned/repeated like CGI
- finish behavior ignores substrate interaction
- the scene is visually impressive but teaches nothing about the paper

## Homepage use

Homepage imagery should not try to show every family equally.

Use:
- one hero sheet study
- one four-family material spectrum
- one featured material
- one surface-to-structure transition
- one rare full-bleed material passage

The homepage remains editorial. The Paper Atlas carries breadth.

## Paper Atlas use

Index cards:
- image first
- family + GSM as metadata
- material name
- one-sentence material character
- no decorative card container

Detail pages:
- large 5/7 split hero
- gallery evidence sequence
- technical register
- print compatibility
- finishing compatibility
- application notes
- provenance state

## Data integrity

Imagery can communicate physical character but must never be used to invent technical facts.

Any GSM, caliper, opacity, brightness, fibre composition, print compatibility, finishing compatibility, or availability claim must come from a supplier source, physical measurement, or another documented evidence source.

Prototype records remain marked as prototype until verified.
